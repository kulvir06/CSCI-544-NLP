# Benchmark Annotation Guide

The 24 rows are **candidates**, not gold labels, until this process is complete.

## Independent review

1. Assign `reviewer-a.csv` and `reviewer-b.csv` to two different team members.
2. Reviewers must work independently and must open the cited official source.
3. For every row, verify the evidence quote, relevant date, explanation, error type,
   gold safety label, expected temporal verdict, and atomic-fact checklist.
4. Set `approve` to `yes` only if all fields are defensible. Put the reviewer's
   full name in `reviewer` and explain corrections or uncertainty in `review_notes`.
5. A reviewer may edit `gold_label`, `error_type`, or
   `expected_temporal_verdict`; do not silently edit the quoted source text.

Label meanings:

- `safe`: the explanation is supported, date-applicable, and preserves every
  critical fact.
- `unsafe`: the explanation contains a wrong version, contradiction, or omission
  that could change the user's decision.
- `abstain`: the supplied evidence cannot establish a safe answer.

## Agreement and freezing

Run the two-reviewer finalizer. It computes raw agreement and Cohen's kappa,
exports disagreements, and freezes only independently approved matching rows:

```bash
python3 -m scripts.finalize_benchmark \
  --draft-jsonl data/reports/benchmark/benchmark-candidates.jsonl \
  --review-csv data/reports/benchmark/reviewer-a.csv \
  --review-csv-b data/reports/benchmark/reviewer-b.csv \
  --disagreements data/reports/benchmark/benchmark-disagreements.csv \
  --output data/reports/benchmark/frozen-benchmark.jsonl
```

Resolve every disagreement by returning to the official source and recording the
reason. Do not tune models on the 16-case test split after it is frozen.

## External audit

Ask a DSO or qualified legal reviewer to audit at least these six subtle cases:
`DEV-03`, `DEV-04`, `TEST-04`, `TEST-06`, `TEST-07`, and `TEST-14`. Record the
reviewer's role, date, decision, and notes. This audit must be performed by a real
reviewer; it is not generated by the system.
